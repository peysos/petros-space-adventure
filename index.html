CHANGE LOG

A scrollable history of every tracked Petros Space Adventure release.

VERSION 0.9

Venus fights back.

VENUS WAVES

  • Waves 6 to 9 now escalate on their own: skimmers fire faster and lead your ship further ahead every wave, and from the middle of the chapter they add a straight third blade so the pair can no longer be split.
  • Acid Skimmers dodge. From wave 7 they read your shots in flight and slip sideways out of the line instead of sitting there.
  • Furnace Blooms walk toward your column, so their seeds start their run from above you.
  • The seed is a hunter now: it steers at your ship the whole way in, arms with a flash and a chirp when it gets close, and bursts along your bearing — one shard always straight at you, six of them at wave 9. Fuse is a third shorter and it leaves the bloom faster.

THE VENUS FIGHT

  • New attack — HUNTER: seeker darts fired one at a time that steer for a long window before committing. It is the only pattern that follows you into a corner.
  • Venus reads you. Hugging a wall pulls the sweep and the pressure ring, standing still pulls the lightning and the seekers, sitting low pulls the dive — and the planet leans much harder toward a ship pinned against an edge.
  • The final phase never gives a single-pattern breather again: it always chains, and the gap between patterns is shorter.

VERSION 0.8

Six new supers & a real Venus sky.

SUPER ATTACKS

  • STAR (45) throws a giant star that ricochets off every wall for six seconds.
  • MIRROR (55) bounces enemy fire off your hull for five seconds and sends it hunting the nearest enemy. It is the slowest super in the game to charge.
  • DRONE (40) launches a warhead you steer with the arrow keys until it hits something; your guns come back the moment it detonates.
  • DECOY (30) drops a 3 HP hologram of your ship that everything in the arena shoots at instead of you, then explodes.
  • FIRST-AID (55) heals four hearts, and RADIANT ORB (25) plants a small sun that sprays fire for four seconds and vaporises anything that touches it.

VENUS

  • Rebuilt the Venus sky: a burning sun behind the haze, four scrolling cloud decks with lit rims and rolling swells, ash and embers climbing the thermals, and cloud-to-cloud lightning.
  • Redrew every Venus projectile — swept sulfur blades, barbed acid darts, spiked seeds with a visible fuse and sharper heat shards, all trailing burning air. No wave, count or timer was touched.

VERSION 0.7

Both bosses, rebuilt.

BOSS FIGHTS

  • Mercury and Venus now run three phases, stepping at two thirds and one third health: every attack timer speeds up and the planet visibly tears.
  • Mercury has 110 health and, from phase 2, throws homing chips of itself that must be shot down or dodged.
  • Venus has 210 health, six attacks instead of four — adding a sweeping searchlight and a body slam down your column — picked at random and chained together in the later phases.
  • Venus's clouds churn and drift, and every attack lights the planet in its own colour before it fires so the harder patterns stay readable.

SCREENS

  • Reverted the Venus wave changes: waves 6 to 9 are back exactly as they were.
  • Loading screen is back to LOADING, and the Mercury reward lock is all grey with a proper latch that strains twice before it gives.
  • Reward buttons now toggle — press again to put back what you had. Loadout icons beside the super meter are smaller, spaced further apart and lined up with the bar.
  • Centred the GAME OVER stack, and the retry button no longer highlights itself unless you reach for the keyboard.

VERSION 0.6

Venus, top to bottom.

VENUS

  • New wave 10 boss: Venus, with 150 health, retrograde cloud decks, a polar vortex, molten fissures that open as it takes damage, and its own heavier theme.
  • Four Venus attacks — an acid downpour with a walking gap, a backwards spiral, warned lightning columns, and a pressure ring with one way out. Each gets harder past half health.

BALANCE & UI

  • Bomb now deals 15 boss damage across a 160 pixel blast, and every super costs noticeably more meter to charge.
  • The equipped gun and super icons are sized and aligned to the super meter, and hovering one shows a quiet card with its stats.
  • Mercury Rewards now offers a grey EQUIP button for Tech.0 and the Grey Ship, and the menu no longer carries the unlocked notice.

VERSION 0.5

Venus patterns & reward polish.

WHAT'S NEW

  • Centered loadout icons without boxes, and Petros Games presents..... on pre-game loading.
  • A larger Mercury Rewards title with a grey gift, smoother opening lock, and looping reward music.
  • Wider Venus formations, curved crescent pairs from Skimmers, and timed Furnace Bloom seeds that burst into diagonal shards.

VERSION 0.4

Armory selection polish.

LOADOUT

  • Uniform book heights, no Equipped tags, and complete glowing outlines on selected and hovered spines.
  • Removed duplicate description stats; Tech.0 now fires every two seconds. Equipped gun and super icons appear beside the super meter.
  • Loadout names use their weapon and super colors, with the plus sign matching the ship.
  • Large icons float gently; separate left/right controls switch the illustration area between icon and damage stats.

VERSION 0.3

Book spines & reward reveals.

PRESENTATION

  • A separate opening-lock screen reveals Tech.0 and the Grey ship before the Mercury victory loadout. Removed the Grey Ship Equipped button.
  • Rebuilt the armory as narrow, icon-only book spines with binding details, empty shelf space, arcade typography, and folded page controls.
  • Fixed missing colors in enlarged weapon icons and removed the Flight Manual label.
  • Game Over is now red with the regular stars retained and the large warp sparkles removed.

VERSION 0.2

The Flight Armory & Mercury update.

ARMORY & COMBAT

  • New book-style weapon and super shelves, illustrated descriptions, and animated page turns. Tech.0 uses a standalone cyan lightning bolt.
  • Tech.0 now fires once every three seconds. Aim changes can no longer bypass firing cooldowns.
  • Bomb blasts reach 210 pixels and deal 24 damage to Mercury.
  • Mercury has more expressive anger, hit reactions, attack wind-ups, and defeat poses.
  • First-time Mercury rewards open with an unlocking animation before the armory appears.

SCREENS & TOUCH

  • Added touch movement, aim/fire, Super, Shrink, and Pause controls for phones and tablets.
  • Improved responsive screen sizing, scrollable panels, safe-area spacing, and portrait/landscape layouts.

VERSION 0.1

The first tracked gameplay and presentation update.

PLAYER & COMBAT

  • Set Blaster and Shield to yellow, Charge to orange, Cone to green, Bomb to blue, and Technology to purple in the selector and combat.
  • Rebalanced super gain, shrink-mode firing, and the Charge weapon's three power tiers.
  • Guaranteed complete three-shot Cone bursts and added an energized Charge wind-up with a flaming full-charge round.
  • Added three visibly larger Charge-ball tiers and the cyan Tech.0 chain-lightning primary.
  • Reworked Tech.0 from the concept art: 3 impact damage, four 0.5-damage chain hops, and a one-second firing cycle.
  • Raised Shield's cost and shortened its protection window, while giving Bomb a layered shockwave detonation.
  • Chargers now pursue the player continuously as projectile-free contact threats.

MERCURY

  • Improved tracking, attack variety, telegraphs, facial animation, and arena placement.
  • Refreshed the boss health display and Mercury-destroyed victory presentation with a clearer heart reward.
  • Added a dedicated defeat screen where a more sinister laughing Mercury delivers his victory line.
  • Beating Mercury now permanently unlocks the Grey Ship and Tech.0, with a clear lock prompt beforehand.
  • Introduced Venus's sulfur-cloud environment, Acid Skimmers, and Furnace Blooms across post-Mercury waves 6–9.

MENUS & AUDIO

  • Added centered, expandable audio settings, pause-menu controls, and ship-color keyboard focus.
  • Renamed the beam super to Technology and gave every loadout tile its own matching palette.
  • Moved additional primaries into an expandable section so future weapons stay inside the selector at every screen size.
  • Added responsive phone and tablet layouts, safe-area support, dual touch sticks, and dedicated Super, Shrink, and Pause controls.
