CHANGE LOG

A scrollable history of every tracked Petros Space Adventure release.

VERSION 0.1

The first tracked gameplay and presentation update.

PLAYER & COMBAT

  • Set Blaster and Shield to yellow, Charge to orange, Cone to green, Bomb to blue, and Technology to purple in the selector and combat.
  • Rebalanced super gain, shrink-mode firing, and the Charge weapon's three power tiers.
  • Guaranteed complete three-shot Cone bursts and added an energized Charge wind-up with a flaming full-charge round.
  • Added three visibly larger Charge-ball tiers and the cyan Tech.0 chain-lightning primary.
  • Reworked Tech.0 from the concept art: 3 impact damage, four 0.5-damage chain hops, and a one-second firing cycle.
  • Raised Shield's cost and shortened its protection window, while giving Bomb a layered shockwave detonation.
  • Chargers now pursue the player continuously as projectile-free contact threats.

MERCURY

  • Improved tracking, attack variety, telegraphs, facial animation, and arena placement.
  • Refreshed the boss health display and Mercury-destroyed victory presentation with a clearer heart reward.
  • Added a dedicated defeat screen where a more sinister laughing Mercury delivers his victory line.
  • Beating Mercury now permanently unlocks the Grey Ship and Tech.0, with a clear lock prompt beforehand.
  • Introduced Venus's sulfur-cloud environment, Acid Skimmers, and Furnace Blooms across post-Mercury waves 6–9.

MENUS & AUDIO

  • Added centered, expandable audio settings, pause-menu controls, and ship-color keyboard focus.
  • Renamed the beam super to Technology and gave every loadout tile its own matching palette.
  • Moved additional primaries into an expandable section so future weapons stay inside the selector at every screen size.
